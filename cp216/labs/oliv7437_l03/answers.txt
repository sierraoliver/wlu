Task 1

Task 2
- The address of COUNTER is 1020

Task 3
- By looking at the LED device indicators, when changing the DELAY_TIME it adjusts how fast the LED's are rotated. When increasing the time, it moves slower, and by reducing the time, it moves faster. When adjusting the LED_BITS, it adjusts which individual LEDs are active. If the bit is a 1, the LED is lit and when it is 0 it is not. Thus, for the original bits 0x0F0F0F0F it has a group for 4 active LED's followed by 4 inactive bits repeatedly.
